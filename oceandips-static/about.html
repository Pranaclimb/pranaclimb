Annie Anderson

Founder — Ocean Dips & Pranaclimb Ltd  ·  Cornwall

About Annie

Annie Anderson is a qualified rock climbing instructor, registered yoga teacher, independent researcher, and breathwork specialist with over 35 years of worldwide climbing experience.

She is the founder of Ocean Dips — private guided coastal experiences on Cornwall's spectacular north coast — and Pranaclimb Ltd, a breath-led performance system for climbers and athletes that she has developed over decades of coaching, research, and practice.

She lives with her husband Mark and daughter Annalisa beside the sea cliffs of Cornwall, where she climbs, swims in the Atlantic, and practises breathwork and yoga throughout the year.

The Story

Annie has spent over three decades climbing, coaching, and researching at the intersection of breath, movement, and the natural world. A personal 4-year journey through Stage 4 Kidney Cancer and surgery deepened her understanding of what the ocean, cold water, and nature can do — not just for performance, but for recovery, resilience, and wellbeing. That lived experience is woven into everything Ocean Dips offers.

🌊 Ocean Dips

Private guided coastal experiences combining breathwork, cold-water immersion, coastal walking, and sea cliff climbing on Cornwall's wild north coast. Each experience is shaped by the tides, weather, and the rhythm of the Atlantic.

🫁 Pranaclimb

A breath-led climbing performance system bridging sports physiology and practical coaching. Annie's research has been published on SportRxiv and ResearchGate.

📖 The Book

Author of Pranaclimb: Love, Breathe, Climb — combining science, breathwork, and elite insights with unique practices for climbers at every level.

🌿 Cornwall

Living beside the sea cliffs of Gwithian, Annie swims daily in the Atlantic, walks the coastal paths, and shares the magic of this extraordinary coastline with every guest.

The Philosophy

Annie believes that breath, cold water, movement, and nature are among the most powerful tools we have for resetting the nervous system, building resilience, and reconnecting with what matters.

Ocean Dips is the natural extension of everything she has lived, researched, and practised — bringing the science of the Pranaclimb methodology to the ocean and the wild Cornish coast.
