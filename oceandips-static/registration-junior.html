Acknowledgement of Risk

Ocean Dips and Pranaclimb Ltd activities — including cold-water immersion, breathwork, and climbing — take place in coastal and outdoor environments where inherent risks exist. These include but are not limited to: slips and falls on uneven or wet terrain, changing weather and sea conditions, physical and mental demands, and the residual risks inherent to adventure activities.

Pranaclimb Ltd holds full professional indemnity and public liability insurance with Insure4Sport (Underwritten by Aviva Insurance Limited). Annie Anderson holds a current first aid certificate and carries a full first aid kit on all sessions.

Under 18s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times. As the parent or legal guardian, you are asked to acknowledge that adventure activities carry inherent risk and to consent to your child's participation on that basis.

Signing this form does not compromise your or your child's legal rights, nor does it release Annie Anderson or Pranaclimb Ltd from their duty of care.
