Acknowledgement of Risk

Ocean Dips and Pranaclimb Ltd activities — including cold-water immersion, breathwork, and climbing — take place in coastal and outdoor environments where inherent risks exist. These include but are not limited to: slips and falls on uneven or wet terrain, changing weather and sea conditions, physical and mental demands beyond your usual experience, and the residual risks inherent to adventure activities.

Pranaclimb Ltd holds full professional indemnity and public liability insurance with Insure4Sport (Underwritten by Aviva Insurance Limited). Annie Anderson holds a current first aid certificate and carries a full first aid kit on all sessions.

All reasonable steps are taken to ensure your safety. Signing this form does not compromise your legal rights, nor does it release Annie Anderson or Pranaclimb Ltd from their duty of care. You are asked to acknowledge that adventure activities carry inherent risk and to commit to contributing to your own and others' safety throughout the experience.
